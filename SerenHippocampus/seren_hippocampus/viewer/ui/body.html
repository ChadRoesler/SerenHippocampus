A sleep reads the day's short-term memories and proposes what should become long-term. A tend picks up anything the reviewer sent back and tries again. Both are safe to press; a running one refuses a second.

Last tend

Drafts the hippocampus has proposed and the reviewer has not finished. Each operation is approved or denied on its own, in SerenMemory (its viewer, or the model through its tools). Nothing here changes long-term memory until it is approved.

How the drafting is going, per model, and every sleep end to end: the brief that opened it, each attempt, what the reviewer said, and what landed. Use it to catch the small model drifting, and to judge a model swap on numbers instead of a feeling.

Replay a draft on another model

Sends the exact prompts a draft was built from to a candidate model and shows both side by side, with the verdicts the original got and what finally landed. Nothing is submitted to Memory. Start the candidate's server first (on its own port), then give its address.

Chains

The last sleeps and tends this service ran, newest first, with what each one did or why it stopped.

Events

What the service told the world: a draft submitted, a sleep that failed, a chain that ended, a purge. With a webhook configured each one says whether it was delivered.
